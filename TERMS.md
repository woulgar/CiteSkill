# CiteSkill Terms

Effective September 25, 2026.

CiteSkill is provided as open-source software under the [MIT License](LICENSE). The license governs use, modification, and redistribution of the software. The software is provided without warranty, as stated in that license.

Citation records and contribution percentages are declarations by the people maintaining a repository. CiteSkill validates their format and some Git references; it does not verify authorship, intellectual-property rights, or whether a source was actually used. Review records before publishing them.

CiteSkill's attribution convention encourages credit for direct sources. It does not create a separate legal obligation for downstream projects. Users are responsible for complying with the licenses and policies of any sources they cite or use.

Questions and support requests can be filed at [CiteSkill issues](https://github.com/woulgar/CiteSkill/issues).
