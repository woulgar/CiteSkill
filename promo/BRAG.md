# CiteSkill launch video

The 20-second English launch video is [`citeskill-launch.mp4`](citeskill-launch.mp4), with a matching [`poster`](citeskill-launch.jpg). It shows all supported source kinds: agents, models, skills, plugins, apps, MCP servers, and projects. Its example names only direct sources used in CiteSkill: Codex, GPT Sol, plugin-creator, Brag, and Hyperframes. Agent and model percentages are user estimates, provider percentages derive from model estimates, and the other kinds receive citations without percentages. The commit shown as the work reference is `b3b8902`; the follow-up citation commit carrying `CiteSkill-Refs:` is `6e09daf`.

## Direct production sources

- [Brag](https://github.com/latent-spaces/brag) — the video planning and production skill.
- [Hyperframes](https://github.com/heygen-com/hyperframes) — HTML composition, validation, and MP4 rendering.
- Codex (GPT Sol) — storyboard, composition, original synthesized audio, and review.

The soundtrack was generated locally for this video. No third-party music is included. The video makes no claim that an official marketplace listing or the public repository is live.

Suggested share copy: "CiteSkill credits the agents, models, skills, plugins, apps, MCP servers, and projects behind your work. Estimate agent and model shares; cite every other direct source."
